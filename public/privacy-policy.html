Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Football Wordle ("we", "our", "this site") is a free browser-based word game for football fans. This page explains how we handle your data — in short, we collect none.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. No name, email address, IP address, or device identifier is recorded by us.

2. Local storage

Your game stats (win streak, guess distribution, total games played) are saved in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear your browser data.

3. Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager) to collect anonymous usage data, including pages visited, session duration, and general geographic region (country level). Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish users. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored by us. Data is processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses data from sites that use our services .

5. Cookies

This site uses the following types of cookies:

  • Functional cookies: We store your game statistics (win streak, guess distribution, total games) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Preference cookies: We store your cookie consent decision in localStorage.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga and _gid) to track anonymous usage data as described above.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense may set cookies to serve personalised advertisements as described above.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect game functionality.

6. Third-party services

This site uses Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described above. No other social-media trackers or third-party tracking scripts are embedded. The site is hosted on Netlify, whose privacy policy also applies to your connection.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at webgames594@gmail.com.
